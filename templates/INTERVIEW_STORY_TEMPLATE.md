# My 60-second project story

Practise out loud 5 times, until you can say it without notes.

1. **Problem:** "The [stakeholder] wanted to know [question]."
2. **Data:** "I used [data] — [size, number of tables]."
3. **What I did:** "I [cleaned X], [built Y], and found [main finding with a number]."
4. **Result:** "I recommended [action], which could [impact]."

## Be ready for these questions

- What was the hardest part of the data, and how did you handle it?
- Why did you choose that metric or definition?
- How do you know your numbers are right? (Row counts, unique keys, totals you checked.)
- What would you do differently, or next?
- If the stakeholder disagreed with your finding, what would you do?
